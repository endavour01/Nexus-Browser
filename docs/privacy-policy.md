## 1. Our privacy commitment

NEXUS Browser is an independent browser project built around user choice, transparency, and control.

This Privacy Policy explains what information NEXUS Browser may handle, where that information goes, why it is needed, how long it may be retained, and what choices you have.

Our guiding principles are:

- **Local by default:** Information should remain on your device whenever a feature can reasonably operate locally.
- **No hidden collection:** We will explain the information required by online services and optional features.
- **User choice:** Optional data processing should be clearly disclosed and controllable wherever technically possible.
- **No sale of personal data:** We do not intend to sell users' personal information.
- **Honest security claims:** We will not describe a feature as encrypted, anonymous, or completely private unless its implementation supports that claim.

This policy must reflect the actual released version of NEXUS Browser. A feature described as optional or unavailable must not begin collecting information without the appropriate implementation, disclosure, and user controls.

## 2. Who is responsible for your information?

NEXUS Browser is developed independently and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or operated by Brave Software, Google, Apple, Microsoft, Spotify, or other third-party providers unless explicitly stated.

The developer or legal entity identified above is responsible for the information processed directly through NEXUS services, subject to applicable law.

When you visit a third-party website or use an external service through NEXUS, that provider may independently process your information under its own privacy policy and terms.

## 3. Information NEXUS may process

The information involved depends on the features you use.

### A. Browsing information

Browsing information can include visited URLs, search queries, page titles, browsing history, cookies, downloads, and open-tab information.

Where these features are stored locally, they remain on your device unless you choose an action or feature that sends relevant information elsewhere.

Websites you visit can receive information needed to deliver their content, such as your IP address, browser and device information, and information you submit to them. Your search provider may also receive your search queries.

**Our intended policy:** NEXUS will not send your complete browsing history to its developer for advertising or profiling. Any future feature that transmits URLs, page content, tab details, or browsing activity to an NEXUS-operated server must disclose what is sent, why it is needed, and how long it is retained.

### B. Notes, tasks, and preferences

NEXUS may provide Notes, Todo, reading preferences, themes, and other personal productivity features.

- Locally stored notes and tasks should remain on your device.
- If cloud synchronization is implemented and you enable it, the relevant information may be transferred to the selected cloud provider.
- Your account information and synchronization data may be processed separately.
- Deleting an item locally does not necessarily delete previously synchronized copies or backups immediately.

The application must clearly distinguish local-only storage from cloud-synchronized storage.

### C. Account and authentication information

If you create a NEXUS account using email, Google, Apple, or another supported sign-in provider, the authentication service may process information such as your email address, account identifier, authentication tokens, and sign-in status.

If NEXUS uses Supabase or another cloud provider, that provider may process account and database information on our behalf.

We will not request or store your Google or Apple account password. Authentication must use the supported provider's secure sign-in process.

Account creation, cloud storage, and authentication will only be described as available once they have been implemented and configured. The final policy must name the actual providers in use.

### D. NEXUS Bot and AI features

When you ask NEXUS Bot to answer a question, summarize a webpage, explain text, or perform another AI task, the information processed depends on how that feature is implemented.

Depending on the feature, information sent to an AI provider could include:

- Your prompt and conversation context.
- Selected text or webpage content you ask it to analyze.
- Relevant tab titles or URLs, if the feature explicitly requires them.
- Technical request information needed to provide the service.

AI requests may be processed by an external provider under its own terms and privacy policy. That provider may have its own retention and model-training practices.

NEXUS must clearly identify the relevant provider and disclose its data practices before transmitting personal or webpage information. We will not promise that AI conversations are never stored or used for model improvement without verifying the provider's actual terms and configuration.

Whenever practical, AI features should transmit only the information needed to fulfill the request.

### E. Search, translation, and other online services

Search suggestions, translation, currency conversion, news, dictionary lookups, and similar features may contact external services.

These services may receive the query, text, currency pair, requested content, IP address, or other information necessary to respond.

NEXUS should disclose the relevant provider and offer a way to disable optional services where practical. A lookup of selected text may disclose that text to the service handling the request.

### F. NEXUS Connect and NEXUS Music

When you open services such as Spotify, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Discord, Google Meet, or other third-party platforms through NEXUS, those platforms may receive your requests and process information according to their own policies.

Signing into a third-party website does not automatically give NEXUS access to your account password or private account data. However, information handled by an embedded website or an authorized integration depends on the actual integration.

NEXUS does not control the privacy practices, content, availability, or security of independent third-party services.

### G. VPN, security, and protection features

If NEXUS offers a VPN or connects to a provider such as Windscribe, that provider may process information required to establish and operate the connection. This may include connection metadata and your IP address, depending on its implementation and policies.

A VPN connection must not be advertised as active unless the connection has actually been established.

Website blocking, anti-tracking, pop-up protection, and malicious-site protection may work differently depending on the protection engine and its data sources. Some protections may rely on third-party blocklists, reputation services, or safe-browsing providers.

We will disclose material third-party processing and will not claim that every threat is blocked.

### H. Diagnostics and technical information

A browser may need limited technical information to identify crashes, errors, and compatibility issues.

If NEXUS implements diagnostics, we will document the categories collected, the recipient, the purpose, the retention period, and whether collection is optional. Crash reports can sometimes contain sensitive information, so they should be reviewed and minimized before transmission.

We will not claim that NEXUS collects no analytics or diagnostics until the application and its dependencies have been audited to confirm that.

## 4. Information we do not intend to collect for advertising

NEXUS is not intended to build advertising profiles from users' browsing histories, sell their browsing histories, or sell their personal information.

We will not intentionally collect complete browsing histories for the developer's own advertising purposes.

This commitment does not prevent websites, search providers, VPN providers, or other third parties from processing information independently when you use their services. Nor does it override a lawful obligation that applies to the responsible operator.

Any future advertising, analytics, or personalization feature must be disclosed separately and implemented with appropriate privacy controls.

## 5. Cookies, local storage, and permissions

Websites opened in NEXUS may store cookies or other website data. NEXUS may also use local application storage to retain settings, notes, tasks, and other feature data.

Website cookies and application storage are not necessarily the same thing. Clearing one does not guarantee that the other has been cleared.

If NEXUS requests permission to use your microphone, camera, location, notifications, files, or clipboard, that permission should be used only for the relevant functionality. A website may also request permissions through the browser.

Grant permissions only to websites and features you trust.

## 6. Private or incognito browsing

Private browsing is intended to reduce the information retained in the browser's normal local history after the private session ends.

It does **not** guarantee complete anonymity. Depending on the browser implementation, websites, internet service providers, employers, network administrators, DNS providers, VPN providers, and other services may still be able to observe or infer activity.

Private browsing does not automatically prevent websites from tracking you, make downloads disappear, erase information submitted to websites, or remove records held by third parties.

The final release documentation must explain precisely what NEXUS Private Browsing does and does not retain.

## 7. How information may be shared

Information may be processed by:

1. **Service providers:** Authentication, hosting, database, AI, search, diagnostics, and other providers necessary for a feature you use.
2. **Websites you visit:** According to your interactions with those websites.
3. **You-authorized integrations:** Services you explicitly connect or sign into.
4. **Legal and security processes:** Where disclosure is required by applicable law or is reasonably necessary to address fraud, abuse, or a serious security issue.

We do not intend to sell personal information. Any service-provider arrangement must be assessed for appropriate privacy, security, and retention protections.

The exact providers and categories of information must be listed in the released policy once the production architecture is known.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

We aim to retain information only for as long as it is necessary for the stated purpose.

- **Local browsing data:** Managed through the browser's available history, cookie, cache, and storage controls.
- **Notes and tasks:** Retained according to the local or cloud-storage configuration.
- **Account information:** Retained while the account is active and as required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.
- **Cloud copies and backups:** May remain for a limited period after deletion, according to the provider's verified retention schedule.
- **Diagnostics and support requests:** Retained according to the published retention period for that service.

We will publish specific retention periods where information is collected by NEXUS or its service providers. We will not invent a deletion timeline or promise immediate deletion from backups unless the relevant system supports it.

## 9. Security

We use, or intend to implement, appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by NEXUS.

Depending on the actual architecture, these measures may include secure authentication, encrypted network connections, restricted database access, access-control policies, secure handling of credentials, and protection against unauthorized access.

No software, storage system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep NEXUS updated, protect their devices, and use strong authentication.

We will not describe local storage as encrypted, cloud sync as end-to-end encrypted, or account data as inaccessible to administrators unless those claims have been technically verified.

## 10. Children's privacy

NEXUS is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Where age-related restrictions or parental consent requirements apply, the service must comply with them.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to an NEXUS-operated service inappropriately, contact us using the address below.

## 11. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the processing of your personal data. You may also have rights to withdraw consent, object to certain processing, or raise a complaint.

Available choices may include:

- Managing or clearing browsing data.
- Disabling optional features and permissions.
- Managing connected accounts and synchronization.
- Requesting account deletion, where account services are available.
- Contacting us with a privacy request.

We will respond to valid requests according to applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or legitimate operational reasons.

## 12. International data transfers

Third-party providers may process information in countries outside your own. Where NEXUS directly arranges such processing, we will take steps required by applicable law and disclose material arrangements where appropriate.

The actual locations and safeguards must be verified against the providers used in the released product.

We will update the effective date and provide notice of material changes through an appropriate channel. Where required, we will seek consent before introducing new processing that requires it.
